The app will be used by company mechanics, drivers, dispatch, and management to track preventive maintenance, inspections, defects, repairs, and vehicle compliance for semi-trucks, trailers, and refrigerated trailers.
Do not include parts inventory or parts purchasing. Parts will be sourced externally, but the mechanic must be able to record the vendor name, part description, invoice number, cost, and upload the invoice against the work order.

Administrator
Manage users and permissions
Add trucks, trailers, mechanics, and drivers
Configure PM intervals
View all maintenance and compliance reports
Maintenance Manager
Create and assign work orders
Review defects
Approve repairs
Place or remove vehicles from service
Review completed work
Mechanic
View assigned jobs
Perform PM1, PM2, greasing, inspections, and repairs
Complete digital checklists
Record measurements, notes, labour hours, parts used, and vendor invoices
Upload before-and-after photos
Mark defects as repaired, deferred, restricted, or out of service
Digitally sign completed work
Driver
Complete daily vehicle inspections
Report defects with photos and location
View vehicle maintenance status
Receive confirmation when the vehicle is released

Driver defect workflow:
Driver selects vehicle
Records defect, location, odometer, photos, and severity
Maintenance manager reviews it
Work order is created
Vehicle is marked available, restricted, or out of service
Mechanic repairs and signs
Manager performs final release
Out-of-service controls:
Immediately remove the vehicle from dispatch availability
Require repair documentation
Require mechanic signature
Require supervisor approval before release
Keep a full audit trail of who changed the status
